# Accounts — Risks & Open Questions

Source: `docs_from_blueprint/module/Accounts/09-risks-and-open-questions.md` (19 consolidated risk
findings: 2 Critical, 4 High, 1 Medium-High, 6 Medium, 4 Low-Medium, 2 Low; 46 deduplicated open
questions grouped by theme).

**AccountStatement note**: findings #3 (finance-charge divisor divergence), #10 (duplicated
suppression threshold), #11 (B2B statement permission bypass — `isPermitted('AccountStatement',
'ListView')` skipped for `requestfrom=b2bfrontend`), and #13 (full/quick discount-text drift) in the
source are statement-specific and now also carried in AccountStatement's own risk register at
`docs_from_blueprint/module/AccountStatement/09-risks-and-open-questions.md`. Per this task's scope,
**finding #11 (the AccountStatement `isPermitted` permission-bypass finding) is deliberately excluded
from this file's Risk Register below** — it belongs to AccountStatement's own permissions/risk
documentation, not Accounts'. The other three (finance-charge divisor divergence, duplicated
suppression threshold, discount-text drift) are genuine Accounts-module findings independent of the
statement-module boundary and are retained below, since the source's own register treats Accounts'
copy as the complete one.

## Anti-hallucination discipline

Per this project's documentation discipline, nothing above should be silently resolved by guessing
in downstream stages. Every item stays open until explicitly answered by a subject-matter expert or
a targeted follow-up read of the cited legacy source. The full 46-item enumerated open-questions list
(of which the 25 above are the source's own representative examples across its seven theme groups)
is available at `blueprint/module/Accounts/08-consolidation-review.md` §4 if a downstream stage needs
an item not captured above.
